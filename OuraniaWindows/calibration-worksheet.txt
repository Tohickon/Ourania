RULES - fix these before you start, apply identically to all:
  1. Record a planet only if he says it is in THIS chart. Skip planets named
     purely as theory ("the out-of-sect malefic usually...") unless he then
     says which planet that is here.
  2. Named by house or sign counts. Record the planet.
  3. An angle alone does not count unless he names its ruler.
  4. Cap at 4. If more, take those named first.
  5. Cannot tell? LEAVE BLANK and skip. A skipped chart costs one row;
     a guessed one corrupts the result.
