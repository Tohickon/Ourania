=nessus sextile chiron
Old hurts about being used or pushed around can be put to rest here, but only if you talk about them.
Each of you arrived with a story about someone who went too far. Nessus in sextile to Chiron gives the pair a chance to tell those stories without being pitied or doubted. It takes a deliberate evening, phones away, and a promise not to fix anything. Skip that and the same sore subjects keep getting stepped around at dinner.

=nessus square chiron
A sore spot in one of you keeps getting pressed by the other's need to stay on guard.
The row usually starts small: a question about where someone was, or a door shut a little too firmly. Because Nessus squares Chiron, a request for space lands as rejection, and a show of concern lands as control. Whoever was hurt worst in the past reacts first. It happens again the following month, over something just as minor, until you learn to name the old injury out loud.

=nessus opposition jupiter
One of you says yes to everything and the other reads the small print.
The generous one books the trip, lends the money, and invites the cousin to stay. The wary one asks who benefits and what happens if it goes wrong. Nessus opposite Jupiter puts trust and suspicion in two different chairs at the same table. Loans to relatives are the classic flashpoint. Each of you is half right, which is why neither backs down.

=nessus sextile mars
Anger at being pushed around becomes useful once the two of you point it at a job.
There is a lot of fight in this pair, and with Nessus sextile Mars it does not have to go into rows. Pick something that needs muscle and nerve: a dispute with a landlord, a garden wall, a long overdue complaint. Do it together and you both feel stronger. Left without a target, the same heat comes out as snapping at each other over the washing up.

=nessus square mars
Tempers and territory collide, and neither of you will be told what to do.
Someone moves the tools, takes the car without asking, or gives an order in the wrong tone. Nessus square Mars turns that into a contest about who is in charge, fast. The one who feels cornered hits back hardest. Doors get slammed and then nobody speaks until bedtime. The pattern repeats most when you are both tired and the house is a mess.

=nessus opposition mars
A push from one of you meets a wall from the other.
When Mars wants something done now, Nessus on the far side hears a threat and digs in. So one partner drives and the other blocks, and the roles can swap from one week to the next. Who decides about the car, the weekend, the builders? You tend to settle it by each ruling a separate patch of the house and staying off the other's.

=nessus sextile moon
Home can become the one place where nobody is ever leaned on, if you set it up that way.
Say what you each need to feel safe indoors: a locked bathroom door, no shouting, relatives who phone before they visit. With Nessus in sextile to the Moon these small agreements do a great deal, but someone has to raise them. The pair that never has the conversation ends up guessing, and guessing wrong on the worst possible evening.

=nessus opposition neptune

A bill goes unpaid, a story does not add up, and the questions start. Neptune drifts off or changes the subject. Nessus, across the chart, takes the vagueness as a lie and presses harder. That pressure produces more fog. Money and drink are the usual subjects. Things calm down only when something is written on paper where both of you can see it.

=nessus opposition pluto
Each of you is afraid of being controlled, and each of you is doing some controlling.
Passwords, bank logins, who rang and why: with Nessus opposite Pluto these become tests of loyalty. One partner watches and the other hides, then you trade places. Nobody says what they are really frightened of. It is a hard pattern to live in, and outsiders rarely see it. The grip loosens on the days when you have a common enemy to face instead.

It is dull work for a wet Sunday. Do it and a whole class of future arguments never happens. Put it off and every bill reopens the question of who can be relied on.

=nessus opposition saturn

Silence follows, sometimes for days. What looks like laziness on one side and coldness on the other is really two people protecting themselves in the only way they know.
