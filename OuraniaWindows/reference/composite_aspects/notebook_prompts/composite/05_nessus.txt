===== nessus 1 of 10 =====
Using the source titled Ourania writing brief, write the 10 readings listed below. Each describes a permanent feature of the relationship's own chart.
For each reading give exactly three parts, in this order, as plain text:
1. the heading, copied exactly as I list it, on a line by itself
2. one lead sentence on its own line that states the whole dynamic plainly
3. one paragraph of three to five sentences
Rules: 65 to 90 words per reading. No bullet points, no bold, no quotation marks, no dashes, no citations. Readings that share the same two bodies must use completely different sentences from one another: do not repeat a definition or a closing line. Use the meanings of the bodies and of each aspect given in the brief. Write nothing before the first heading or after the last paragraph.
Avoid these habits, which make the readings sound machine-made: do not begin a paragraph by naming the aspect (A square between, Under a trine between, An opposition between); do not end on advice that starts with an -ing word (Continuing to, Practicing, Learning to, Maintaining); vary how each reading opens and closes. A sextile is NOT a mild trine: it is a chance that only pays off when the pair makes an effort, and lies idle otherwise, so say what the effort is. Use plain words about ordinary life (meals, money, rows, relatives, work, bed) and do not use: sacred, holy, spiritual, sanctuary, catalyst, evolutionary, profound, dynamic, vibrant, effortless, harmony, journey, destiny. In every reading say in a few words what each of the two bodies stands for.
