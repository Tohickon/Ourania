# Part of Spirit as first body: 85 readings, written by hand.

=spirit conjunction ascendant
What this couple intends is written on its face.
Nobody has to guess what the two of you are about. With the Part of Spirit on the Ascendant, your aims walk into the room before you do: the campaign, the business, the plan to move abroad. That makes you persuasive and easy to recruit for. It also means you are seldom off duty, and friends who only wanted a drink get the full prospectus.

=spirit sextile ascendant
Saying out loud what you are trying to do would bring help you are not getting.
People like the pair of you without quite knowing what you are for. The Part of Spirit in sextile to the Ascendant closes that gap whenever you bother to state the aim plainly, at a dinner, on a form, to the neighbor with the van. Help turns up once it has something to attach to. Keep it private and you stay well liked and unassisted.

=spirit square ascendant
The way you come across keeps working against what you actually mean to do.
You intend to be taken seriously and arrive joking. Or you want a quiet life and somehow chair every meeting. The Part of Spirit squaring the Ascendant puts purpose and manner at odds, so others respond to the wrong signal and you both go home irritated. Which of you gave the false impression this time is a row you could hold in your sleep.

=spirit trine ascendant
Manner and purpose fit, so people understand you at once.
There is no translating to do. With the Part of Spirit trine the Ascendant the couple looks like what it is trying to be, and the right allies recognize you early. Plans get a fair hearing. This comes so readily that you underrate it, and you can be puzzled by pairs who work twice as hard to be believed.

=spirit opposition ascendant
Your sense of purpose keeps turning up in the people you meet.
Opposite the Ascendant the Part of Spirit sits with partners, clients and rivals, so what the two of you are meant to do tends to be shown to you by somebody else: a mentor's remark, a competitor's success. One of you finds that inspiring. The other feels directed from outside and digs in. Whose idea was it really? is asked a good deal in this house.

=spirit conjunction chiron

The couple's purpose and its sore spot are in the same place. Chiron joined to the Part of Spirit means the project, whatever it is, exists because of an old hurt: the school that failed one of you, an illness, a parent's bankruptcy. That gives the work depth and stamina. It also means criticism of the plan lands on the wound, and you both overreact.

=spirit sextile chiron
An old difficulty could be put to use, if you decide to use it.
Chiron in sextile to the Part of Spirit offers a trade. What each of you learned the hard way is worth something to other people, as teaching, as advice, as a service nobody else thought of. Turning it into that takes a conscious choice and a first awkward attempt. Left alone, the experience stays private and merely painful.

=spirit square chiron
Ambition keeps stepping on the tender place.
Every time you push for something, one of you is reminded of not being good enough. Chiron square the Part of Spirit makes goals themselves the trigger: the application, the audition, the business plan. So you set an aim and then flinch from it, and the partner who did not flinch calls it self sabotage. It recurs with each new attempt until the fear is named before the form is filled in.

=spirit trine chiron
You can aim high without reopening old wounds.
Past failures do not have much grip here. With Chiron trine the Part of Spirit the pair treats what went wrong as information, and each of you can be encouraged without being handled. Plans are made in a kind atmosphere. If anything you are too gentle with one another, and a blunt friend is useful when a project needs honest criticism.

=spirit opposition chiron
One of you is driving toward a goal and the other is nursing a hurt.
Onward, says the Part of Spirit. Chiron across from it says wait, something here still aches. So the purposeful partner feels held back and the sore one feels dragged. You swap roles more often than either admits. Progress comes in the weeks when the one with the plan slows down enough to ask what the matter is, and waits for the answer.

With Jupiter on it, the Part of Spirit does not think small. A degree turns into a doctorate, a market stall into a chain, a holiday into a year away. Squaring the Part of Spirit, it adds a second site, a bigger loan, a launch party, until the original purpose is buried under its own extensions. =spirit trine mercury

Words serve intention well here. With Mercury trine the Part of Spirit the couple can state an aim in two sentences, agree on it between yourselves without a scene, and put it to others without confusion. =spirit trine mc

=spirit opposition mc
