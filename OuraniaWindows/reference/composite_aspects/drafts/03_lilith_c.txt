=lilith conjunction ceres
Care in this relationship comes with teeth.
Ceres is the daily tending, the feeding and the fear of loss. Lilith is the part that will not be owned. Together they produce a fierce protectiveness, like an animal guarding its young, along with an equally fierce resistance to being mothered. The two of you look after each other intensely and on your own terms. Trouble comes when care turns into a claim. Help offered here has to be free to be refused.

=lilith sextile ceres
This relationship can look after its rawer needs if it chooses to notice them.
Lilith holds the hungers that are hard to admit, and Ceres is practical nurture. The sextile lets one answer the other. A meal, rest, or simple physical comfort can settle what would otherwise turn into a quarrel, and honesty about need makes the care more accurate. It works when someone speaks up. Unvoiced, the need goes unfed and the care goes to the wrong place.

=lilith square ceres
The wish to be looked after and the refusal to depend collide here.
Ceres gives and holds on. Lilith pulls free. In a square, tending feels like control to the one receiving it, and independence feels like rejection to the one giving it. Arguments attach themselves to food, health, money and who does more. Separation is a sore subject. It helps to ask before helping, and to hear a refusal as a statement about freedom and nothing worse.

=lilith trine ceres
Fierce independence and steady care sit side by side without trouble.
The two of you can nurture each other without anyone feeling smothered, and can go your own ways without anyone feeling abandoned. Lilith keeps the care honest and Ceres keeps the wildness fed. Partings and reunions are taken in stride. The comfortable arrangement can hide a reluctance to need each other very much, so it is worth checking that self-reliance has not quietly replaced closeness.

=lilith opposition ceres
One partner tends and the other will not be tended.
Ceres and Lilith face each other, so the giver of care and the refuser of it become two people. The carer feels unappreciated and the independent one feels crowded. Each privately wants a little of what the other has: the carer some freedom, the free one some looking-after. Saying so changes the pattern more than any amount of effort in the usual roles.

=lilith conjunction eris

The two of you may be a force for honesty in any group you enter. You may also be a source of constant friction, picking at each other when no outside target presents itself. A cause worth the trouble is the best use of it.

=lilith sextile eris

Between such occasions the edge stays sheathed. That restraint is part of the advantage, since a challenge made rarely carries more weight.

=lilith square eris

Lilith will not submit and Eris will not be excluded. Squared, the pair can provoke for the sake of provoking, each stung by a slight the other did not intend. Feelings of being the outsider, even inside the relationship, are common. So are arguments over fairness that neither can drop. The energy is wasted on each other. Directed outward, at something that merits a fight, it is considerable.

=lilith trine eris
Dissent comes naturally to this relationship and rarely costs it much.
The two of you say unpopular things with ease and back each other when the room turns cold. Lilith supplies the independence and Eris the insistence on being heard. Being outsiders together feels like home. That comfort can turn into a pose. A pair that always expects to stand apart may stop noticing when it is welcome, and miss the chance to belong.

=lilith opposition eris
One of you walks away from the table and the other overturns it.
Lilith's response to a wrong is to leave and owe nothing. Eris's response is to stay and make trouble. Opposed, these become two partners with two strategies, each impatient with the other's. One seems cold and the other seems to enjoy the fight. Both are answers to exclusion. Agreeing on which wrongs deserve which response saves the pair from fighting about how to fight.

=lilith conjunction eros

=lilith trine eros

=lilith opposition eros

=lilith trine hygiea
Instinct and wellbeing support each other in this relationship.
The two of you tend to know what you need and to give it without fuss, and the body is treated as an ally. Lilith keeps the care from turning prim and Hygiea keeps the appetite from turning destructive. It is a healthy arrangement in the plain sense. The only caution is against assuming it will look after itself through periods of strain, when instinct is less reliable.

=lilith opposition hygiea
One of you cleans up and the other makes the mess.
Hygiea's concern for order and health faces Lilith's disregard for both. One partner becomes the one who worries about diet, sleep and symptoms, and the other the one who will not be managed. Each exaggerates in response to the other. The worry is usually partly fear and the defiance partly fear as well. Naming what each is afraid of brings the two closer to the middle.

=lilith trine juno
Loyalty and independence coexist comfortably here.

=lilith opposition juno

Juno stands for the committed partner and Lilith for the one who will not be held. Across the relationship, one of you tends to embody steadiness and the other the threat of departure.
