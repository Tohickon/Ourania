# Vesta as first body: 70 readings, written by hand. The last of the composite set.

=vesta conjunction ascendant
You strike people as a couple with something important to get back to.
There is a contained, purposeful air about the pair. With Vesta on the Ascendant you arrive on time, leave when you said you would, and never quite give a party your whole attention. People respect it and feel faintly dismissed. What you are guarding is real. Let visitors see a little of it, or they will assume it is them you are avoiding.

=vesta sextile ascendant
Your dedication could win you allies if you let it show.
Others would help if they knew what you cared about. The Ascendant in sextile to Vesta makes the pair's focus attractive whenever you bring it out: talk about the work, show the workshop, invite one person in. That takes a decision, since your instinct is privacy. Opened a crack, the door admits exactly the kind of people you would want.

=vesta square ascendant
Devotion to your own concerns keeps making you seem cold.
You are thinking about the project and somebody is trying to tell you about their holiday. Vesta squaring the Ascendant sets the pair's inner focus against the ordinary demands of company, and you fail the small tests: the forgotten birthday, the visit cut short. One of you minds how it looks. The other would honestly prefer to be at home.

=vesta trine ascendant
Seriousness sits well on you, and people trust it.
The pair is plainly committed to something and plainly pleasant about it. With Vesta trine the Ascendant, focus does not make you forbidding: you can leave early without offense and say no without a scene. Neighbors know not to ring before ten. It is a happy balance, held without effort, and you underrate how rare it is.

=vesta opposition ascendant
You meet devotion in other people more than you practice it yourselves.
Opposite the Ascendant, Vesta sits with partners and close associates: the dedicated teacher, the friend with a calling, the client who works all hours. The pair admires them and is a little crowded by them. One of you wonders aloud why you have no such single purpose. The answer may be that you keep lending your focus to theirs.

=vesta conjunction chiron
What you tend most faithfully is the place that hurts.
The couple's dedication and its wound are in the same spot. Chiron joined to Vesta gives a life organized round caring for something damaged: a child's condition, a parent's decline, a talent that was never allowed to grow. You are steadfast and it is noble. It is also tiring past what you admit, and you both need someone to look after the carers.

=vesta sextile chiron
A small daily practice could ease something that has ached for years.
Chiron in sextile to Vesta offers healing by repetition. Ten minutes of the exercises. A weekly visit to the grave. The page written every morning. You must set it up and keep it, and nothing dramatic happens. After a season the ache has changed character. Couples wanting a quicker cure look elsewhere and come back to this.

=vesta square chiron
Throwing yourselves into work keeps the hurt from being felt, and from healing.
When it stings, one of you gets busy. Chiron square Vesta makes dedication a hiding place: longer hours, a cleaner house, another qualification. The other partner is left alone with the feeling and resents the closed door. It comes round whenever grief or failure is in the air. Sit down together for ten minutes before picking up the tools.

=vesta trine chiron
Care comes naturally, and it mends things over time.
Someone is always quietly looked after here. With Chiron trine Vesta the pair's steady attention, meals at regular hours, a patient ear, medicine remembered, does more good than either of you realizes. People recover in your spare room. You would call it nothing special. Those who have been in that room would disagree.

=vesta opposition chiron
One of you is devoted to a task and the other is in need.
The work is absorbing and legitimate. Across the chart, Chiron is having a bad week and has not said so. Vesta opposite it means attention is always owed in two directions, and the pair has to choose hourly. The working partner feels interrupted. The hurting one feels like an interruption. A set time for each, kept, is the usual peace.

=vesta conjunction jupiter
You commit yourselves wholeheartedly to something large.
A faith, a cause, a field of study, pursued with enthusiasm. Jupiter on Vesta gives the couple a big devotion and the optimism to sustain it, and you can be inspiring company. You travel for it and spend on it. The weak point is zeal: assuming that what lights you up should obviously light everyone else, and saying so at dinner.

=vesta sextile jupiter
Your dedication would grow if you gave it a wider world.
Jupiter in sextile to Vesta says the practice is sound and a bit enclosed. Take it to a conference. Find the teacher abroad. Read the people who disagree. The pair has to step out deliberately, which is against its habits. What comes back is a larger, more generous version of the thing you already love.

=vesta square jupiter
Too many good causes keep scattering your focus.
You are committed to this, and also to that, and you said yes on Tuesday to a third. Jupiter square Vesta swells the pair's devotions until none gets proper attention. Each new enthusiasm is sincere. One of you collects them and the other counts the neglected ones. Choosing fewer feels like a loss and works like a gain.

=vesta trine jupiter
Faith in what you are doing keeps the work light.
Long hours do not weigh on you. With Jupiter trine Vesta the pair's commitment is buoyed by a sense that it matters and will turn out well, and that mostly proves true. Supporters appear. You rarely suffer burnout. Share some of that ease with people whose devotion is the grim, unrewarded kind.

=vesta opposition jupiter
Staying with one thing pulls against wanting to see everything.
Vesta would keep the lamp lit in one room for life. Jupiter opposite is at the window with a timetable. So one partner deepens and the other widens, and each suspects the other of missing the point. Narrow, says one. Shallow, says the other. A sabbatical every few years, then home again, has kept many such pairs together.

=vesta conjunction mars
You work with an intensity that leaves little room for anything else.
Energy and devotion are welded together. With Mars on Vesta the pair goes at its chosen task hard and daily, whether that is training, building or a business, and thrives on the effort. Rest feels like desertion. Tempers are sharp when the work is interrupted. Desire, too, can get rationed, or else becomes one more thing done with fierce concentration.

=vesta sextile mars
Putting real energy behind your commitment would move it forward fast.
Mars in sextile to Vesta is fuel standing next to a fire that has been burning low. The pair knows what it is dedicated to. It has not lately worked up a sweat for it. Pick a month and push: early starts, a deadline, physical effort. The will is available on request, and never volunteers.

=vesta square mars
Impatience keeps breaking your concentration.
You settle to the task and twenty minutes later someone is slamming a drawer. Mars square Vesta sets the pair's drive against its focus: the urge to act now against the patience the work needs. Projects are rushed, then redone. Frustration turns on whoever is nearest. Hard exercise before the careful work is an old remedy and a good one.

=vesta trine mars
Effort and dedication pull together without strain.
You work hard at what you care about and do not make a drama of it. With Mars trine Vesta the pair has stamina in the right place, a clean way of dividing labor and satisfaction in a finished job. Days are full and tempers are even. Take care that a healthy appetite for work does not eat the weekends by degrees.

=vesta opposition mars
One of you wants to act and the other wants to keep to the practice.
Mars is for the bold stroke: sell it, confront them, start today. Vesta opposite says we have a way of doing things and it takes the time it takes. So drive meets discipline, and each finds the other maddening. The pair is formidable on the days when the impatient one sets the goal and the patient one sets the pace.

=vesta conjunction mercury
Your minds are given over to one subject.
You read about it, talk about it and wake up thinking about it. Mercury on Vesta makes the pair's conversation a kind of shared study, precise and absorbed. You know a great deal. Small talk has withered. Friends learn either to ask about the subject or to bring their own entertainment, and children roll their eyes.

=vesta sextile mercury
Study would deepen what you are dedicated to.
Mercury in sextile to Vesta invites the pair to learn properly about the thing it loves. Take the course. Keep a notebook. Find someone to correspond with. It is a choice, and an easy one to postpone in favor of simply carrying on. Carrying on keeps you where you are. A little reading moves the whole practice up a level.

=vesta square mercury
Chatter and errands keep nibbling away the time you meant to protect.
A quick question becomes forty minutes. Mercury square Vesta fills the hours the pair meant to keep clear with messages, lists and the telephone. Nothing important gets the long quiet it needs. Each of you is guilty and each blames the other's phone. Put both in a drawer and see what an afternoon is like.

=vesta trine mercury
You can explain what you care about clearly and without preaching.
Concentration and conversation are friends in this house. With Mercury trine Vesta the pair thinks in an orderly way about its chosen work and talks about it well, so teaching comes naturally. Notes are kept. Letters get answered. You are the people others ask when they want to understand the thing and not merely admire it.

=vesta opposition mercury
One of you wants silence to work in and the other wants to talk.
Vesta has the door shut. Mercury opposite is outside it with news, a question and something funny from the paper. Neither need is unreasonable. The quiet one feels pecked at. The talkative one feels shut out. Meals with no work at the table, and work hours with no talk, is a treaty that has held in many houses.

=vesta conjunction mc
You are known for your devotion to your work.
The couple's public name rests on dedication. With Vesta on the Midheaven you are the ones who stayed late, kept the standard, gave the calling everything. It earns deep respect. It leaves little that is private, and the home can become a place where two tired people recover between duties. Guard one evening as fiercely as you guard the job.

=vesta sextile mc
Your steadiness is a professional asset you could make more of.
The Midheaven in sextile to Vesta says reliability is exactly what your field is short of. Let it be seen. Take on the long project nobody else will finish. Ask to be judged on years and not weeks. You have to put yourselves forward for that kind of work. Once you do, the pair's patience becomes its reputation.

=vesta square mc
What you are devoted to and what you are paid for keep pulling apart.
The real work is done before breakfast and after supper. Vesta squaring the Midheaven gives the pair a calling on one side and a career on the other, each wanting the best hours. You do both and are tired. One of you says give up the job. The other says give up the dream. Neither will, and the argument keeps you company.

=vesta trine mc
Dedication is noticed and rewarded in your working lives.
You do the job properly and people see it. With Vesta trine the Midheaven the pair's focus translates smoothly into standing: trusted positions, long tenures, work that suits your nature. There is no gap between what you serve and what you are known for. Enjoy that, and take the holiday you are owed.

=vesta opposition mc
Your deepest commitment is to home, and the world keeps wanting you elsewhere.
Opposite the Midheaven, Vesta sits at the foot of the chart, by the actual hearth. The pair's devotion is domestic and private: a household kept well, a family tended. Careers pull the other way and pay the bills. One of you treats the home as the real work. The other brings in the money and would like that counted as devotion too.

=vesta conjunction moon
Home is something you tend with complete seriousness.
The fire is kept in. With the Moon on Vesta the pair's feelings and its devotions are both lodged in the household: meals, beds, the rhythm of the day. It is a deeply cared for place. Emotion can go into chores instead of words, so that a perfectly ironed shirt stands in for I am worried about you.

=vesta sextile moon
A simple household ritual would comfort you both more than you expect.
The Moon in sextile to Vesta asks for one small observance, chosen and kept. Candles at supper. Tea at four. The bed made together. It has to be decided on, because drift is easier. Feelings settle round a fixed point of that sort, and a rushed, irritable house becomes a calmer one within a few weeks.

=vesta square moon
Duty keeps getting in the way of tenderness.
One of you needs a cuddle and the other needs to finish the accounts. The Moon square Vesta sets feeling against focus, and focus has the better excuses. So comfort is postponed and then given stiffly. The neglected partner goes cold in return. It recurs every busy season. Five minutes of undivided attention costs less than the sulk.

=vesta trine moon
Care and routine blend into a very peaceful home.
Nobody goes without clean socks or a kind word. With the Moon trine Vesta the pair's emotional warmth and its sense of order support each other, so the house feels both loved and well run. Children know where they are with you. It is a quiet achievement. An unplanned day now and then would do no harm.

=vesta opposition moon
One of you needs closeness and the other needs to be left to work.
The Moon wants company on the sofa. Vesta opposite wants the door shut until six. So one partner reads withdrawal as rejection, and the other reads a request for attention as sabotage. Neither is meant. It helps to announce the retreat and its end time, and then to come out when promised, in a good temper.

=vesta conjunction neptune
Your devotion has a mystical or artistic cast.
What the pair gives itself to cannot quite be put into words: music, prayer, the sea, a vision of how life could be. Neptune on Vesta makes the dedication pure and the boundaries poor. You will sacrifice a lot for it, sometimes to people who do not deserve it. Keep one foot on the kitchen floor.

=vesta sextile neptune
Imagination would refresh a practice that has gone dry.
Neptune in sextile to Vesta says the pair is faithful to its routine and has forgotten why. Let something in. Play music while you work. Go and look at the thing that first moved you. It is a matter of choosing to, and it feels like slacking. The feeling returns to the form, and the hours stop being merely kept.

=vesta square neptune
Vagueness keeps undermining what you mean to devote yourselves to.
You intend an hour of practice and somehow the hour is gone. Neptune square Vesta dissolves the pair's focus in daydream, screens, a second glass, a muddle about what the commitment actually is. Guilt follows, then a stricter rule, then the same drift. One clear, small, written promise works better than a grand resolve.

=vesta trine neptune
Devotion and inspiration feed each other gently.
You sit down to the work and something more than work happens. With Neptune trine Vesta the pair's dedication has grace in it, whether the form is art, care or contemplation. Hours pass lightly. Others feel a calm in your house they cannot account for. Remember the bills, which are no respecters of atmosphere.

=vesta opposition neptune
Clear dedication faces a tide that would wash it out.
Vesta keeps a small precise flame. Neptune opposite is the fog rolling in: distraction, doubt, other people's needs without limit. One partner holds the line and feels unkind. The other gives way to everything and feels virtuous and lost. Compassion with a timetable is the answer. It sounds harsh and is merciful to all concerned.

=vesta conjunction north_node

Devotion is the direction of travel. With Vesta on the North Node the pair is being asked to choose one thing and tend it: a craft, a household, a service. It does not come easily at first. =vesta sextile north_node
Giving regular time to one commitment would move your lives forward.
The North Node in sextile to Vesta makes a quiet offer. Take up the discipline and growth follows from it, indirectly, the way fitness follows from walking. You have to start and keep going. There is no prize in the first month. The pair that persists finds, a year on, that it has become something it only used to admire.

=vesta square north_node

The practice is faithful and it is in the way. Vesta square the North Node has the pair so occupied with what it already serves that the next stage cannot get a hearing. One of you defends the routine as virtue. The other feels the walls. This returns each time an invitation arrives that does not fit the schedule.

=vesta trine north_node
Steady dedication carries you where you need to go.
You do the work in front of you and it turns out to lead somewhere. With Vesta trine the North Node the pair grows through commitment, without needing upheaval: the long apprenticeship, the patient raising of a family, the same garden for thirty years. It is undramatic. Looking back, the distance covered is considerable.

=vesta opposition north_node

Opposite the North Node, Vesta stands at the South Node, so what the pair tends so faithfully belongs to the past. It is hard to leave a well kept altar. Growth lies elsewhere and asks for some of that loyalty. One of you can feel it and the other calls it betrayal. Bank the old fire properly before you go.

=vesta conjunction pluto

=vesta sextile pluto

=vesta trine pluto

=vesta opposition pluto

=vesta trine saturn

=vesta trine sun
