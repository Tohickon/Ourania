=lilith conjunction ascendant
The untamed side of this relationship is the first thing anyone sees.
Black Moon Lilith, the part of a partnership that will not be managed, sits on the Ascendant, which is how the two of you come across. There is no polite version held in reserve. People meet the intensity, the bluntness and the refusal to play along straight away, and react strongly one way or the other. The pair gains a presence nobody ignores and loses the option of blending in. It helps to decide together which rooms deserve the full force.

=lilith sextile ascendant
This relationship can show its wilder side when it chooses to, and be well received for it.
Lilith is the raw, unaccommodating part of a partnership, and the Ascendant is its outward manner. A sextile links them loosely, so the frankness is available without being constant. Used on purpose, it reads as confidence and wins the pair a reputation for honesty. Left unused, the two of you come across as tamer than you are. The opening is there in social settings and first meetings, and it has to be taken.

Giving the unruly side a deliberate outlet stops it from choosing its own occasions.

=lilith trine ascendant
This relationship wears its untamed side openly and is at ease doing so.
Lilith is the unapologetic streak in a partnership and the Ascendant is the impression it makes. With a trine, the directness comes through in the first impression and people tend to find it magnetic. There is little gap between the private relationship and the public one, which makes the pair hard to shock and hard to shame. The caution concerns other people. What feels natural to the two of you can run over those nearby, and frankness that costs nothing is easy to overuse.

=lilith opposition ascendant

With Lilith opposite the Ascendant she falls on the Descendant, the point of close others, rivals and anyone met face to face. The pair presents one way and keeps attracting the opposite: outsiders who provoke, tempt, or say the unsayable. It is easy to treat these people as the problem. More often they are carrying what the two of you have left out of your own picture. Owning some of that edge yourselves takes the charge out of the encounters.

The pair has to decide to have the difficult conversation, and when it does, it usually goes better than feared.

Outbursts follow hurts that were never named, and fear of rejection sits under a good deal of the fighting. The pattern loosens when the injury is described before the anger arrives. That takes practice and some nerve from both of you.

=lilith trine chiron

The ease has a blind side: being comfortable with a wound is not the same as tending it, and familiar pain can be left exactly where it is.

=lilith opposition chiron

The work is for both of you to hold both: to be hurt and say so, and to be angry and say that too.

=lilith opposition jupiter
