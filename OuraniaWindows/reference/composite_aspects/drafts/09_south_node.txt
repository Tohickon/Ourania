# South Node as first body: 75 readings, written by hand.
# The two Nodes are always opposite each other, so of the five South Node / North Node
# readings only the opposition describes a real chart. The other four are written so that
# they make sense if the app ever shows them.

=south_node conjunction ascendant
You come across as a couple who have known each other for ever.
There is an old ease in how you stand together, and strangers assume a long history even in the first year. With the South Node on the Ascendant the pair leads with habit: familiar roles, practiced jokes, a way of entering a room that never varies. It is comforting to be around. It can also fix you both in an early version of yourselves.

=south_node sextile ascendant
Your long practice at being a couple is an asset you could use more.
People find the pair of you easy because nothing about you is improvised. The South Node in sextile to the Ascendant turns that into an advantage whenever you choose to offer it: host the awkward gathering, take the newcomers under your wing. Familiarity is a skill. Kept at home, it is merely a routine.

=south_node square ascendant
Old habits keep spoiling the impression you mean to give.
You intend to arrive calm and you arrive bickering about the route, as usual. The South Node squaring the Ascendant drags the pair's oldest patterns into public view at exactly the wrong moments. Friends could recite the exchange. One of you is mortified and the other thinks it is nobody's business. It will happen again at the next wedding.

=south_node trine ascendant
What you have always been like together still serves you well.
Nothing about your manner needs fixing. With the South Node trine the Ascendant, the couple's settled ways, how you greet people, who drives, who tells the story, work smoothly and put others at ease. You could go on like this indefinitely. That is the only caution: indefinitely is a long time to make no new first impression.

=south_node opposition ascendant
You keep meeting the past in the people you get close to.
The South Node opposite the Ascendant sits on the Descendant, so partners, clients and close friends tend to be familiar types: the same kind of boss again, another friend who needs rescuing. The pair is drawn to what it already knows how to handle. One of you notices the pattern first. Pointing it out during the rescue is not appreciated.
