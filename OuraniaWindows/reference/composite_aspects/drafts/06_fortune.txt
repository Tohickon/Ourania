=fortune conjunction ascendant
Luck sits right on the surface with this pair: people meet you and things start going well.
Doors open at first meetings. A landlord says yes, a stranger offers a lift, the table by the window comes free. With the Part of Fortune on the Ascendant, simply turning up together is your best asset, and you both know it. The lazy version of this is expecting charm to do work that an email or a deposit should have done.

=fortune sextile ascendant
First impressions can be turned into real advantages if the two of you follow them up.
You get on easily with new people, and the Part of Fortune in sextile to the Ascendant means some of those meetings are worth money, work or a good flat. Nothing arrives by itself, though. Somebody has to ring back, send the thank you note, accept the invitation. Pairs who do find that a little sociable effort pays for a great many holidays.

=fortune square ascendant
What makes life easy for you as a couple is not what you show the world.
In company you play one part, capable or busy or serious, and the Part of Fortune squaring the Ascendant puts your real ease somewhere else entirely. So you turn down the slow Sunday because it does not fit the image, then resent the full diary. The same argument comes round every few months: are we doing this because we like it, or because it looks right?

=fortune trine ascendant
Being yourselves in public happens to be exactly what works.
There is no gap to manage between how you come across and what does you good, so the Part of Fortune trine the Ascendant makes ordinary sociability pay off without anyone trying. Invitations suit you. Favors come back. Because it costs nothing, you rarely notice how much of your comfort comes from people simply liking the pair of you, and you seldom say thanks.

=fortune opposition ascendant
Your good luck tends to arrive through other people, not through your own front door.
A partner's old friend has the job lead. A neighbor knows the builder. With the Part of Fortune opposite the Ascendant it sits on the Descendant, so ease comes when you lean toward others and dries up when you insist on doing everything alone. One of you likes that and the other finds it humiliating. Who asks for the favor is a standing argument.

Chiron in sextile to the Part of Fortune rewards small, scheduled kindnesses of that sort with a calmer house and fewer bad nights. The catch is that neither of you will feel like starting. Start anyway and it carries itself after a month.

Chiron square the Part of Fortune spoils good moments from the inside: the nice restaurant sets off worries about money, the compliment is not believed. The partner who was enjoying it feels cheated. You have this row on holidays more than anywhere, which is a pity, since holidays are the cure.

=fortune trine chiron
Good times and bad memories sit together peacefully here.
One of you can mention the hard year at a birthday dinner and the evening survives it. With Chiron trine the Part of Fortune, pleasure does not have to pretend that nothing ever went wrong, so your comfort is the sturdy kind. Friends in trouble find your sofa a restful place. You could do more with this than you do, but nobody would call it a problem.

=fortune opposition chiron

Luck lands on one side of the bed: the promotion, the clear scan, the good mood. Across from it Chiron holds the partner who has just been reminded of what is wrong. So celebration feels tactless and sympathy feels like a brake. The trick you slowly learn is to let both be true on the same evening, cake and complaint together.

Your luck lives in what you have always known how to do.
Family trades, old friends, the town you grew up in. With the South Node on the Part of Fortune the couple prospers by doing the familiar thing well, and help arrives from people who knew you years ago. It is comfortable and it pays. It also makes leaving very hard, and every new venture has to compete with a sure thing.

=fortune sextile south_node
Old skills are worth dusting off.
The South Node in sextile to the Part of Fortune points at something one of you used to do, a language, an instrument, a trade learned from a parent, and says there is still money or pleasure in it. Getting it out takes a decision and an awkward first week. After that it comes back quickly, as these things do, and earns its keep.

=fortune square south_node
Falling back on old habits keeps costing you.
Under pressure the two of you do what your families did: spend to feel better, or hoard, or say nothing and hope. The South Node square the Part of Fortune makes the reflex feel like comfort while it eats into the real thing. Each of you spots it at once in the other. Seeing it in yourself takes longer, and a certain amount of being told.

=fortune trine south_node
The past is a reliable source of comfort for this pair.
Recipes handed down, a house full of other people's furniture, friends of twenty years. With the South Node trine the Part of Fortune the couple draws ease from what is already known and loved, and it costs you nothing to keep it up. Good. Just notice that nothing new has been added to the list for some while.

=fortune opposition south_node
What does you good now is the opposite of what you are used to.
The Part of Fortune opposite the South Node stands beside the North Node, so luck is found in the unfamiliar direction. Old habits offer comfort that turns out thin. The strange choice, the one your families would not have made, is where things go well. One of you leans forward and one leans back, and the one leaning forward is usually right.

=fortune conjunction vesta
Devotion to one thing is what makes this couple prosper.
A craft, a kitchen, a small business, a child with particular needs. With Vesta on the Part of Fortune the pair finds its ease by tending a single flame carefully, and luck follows concentration. The house may be plain and the hours long, and you are content. Outsiders see sacrifice. You would say you have never wanted the things you gave up.

=fortune sextile vesta
A regular hour of real concentration would improve your fortunes.
Vesta in sextile to the Part of Fortune rewards protected time: the door shut, the phone elsewhere, the same task every morning. It does not need to be much. Kept up, it produces a skill, then a reputation, then an income. The pair has to defend it against each other first, which is harder than defending it against the world.

=fortune square vesta
Dedication keeps crowding out enjoyment.
One of you is at the bench, the desk or the bedside again, and the booked weekend goes by unused. Vesta square the Part of Fortune sets a good cause against a good life, so pleasure feels like slacking and work feels like desertion. The devoted partner calls it necessary. The other counts the cancelled dinners. Both have the numbers on their side.

=fortune trine vesta
Work that matters to you also happens to keep you comfortable.
You would do it anyway. With Vesta trine the Part of Fortune the couple is paid, in money or in peace, for the thing it is most faithful to, and a quiet ordered home comes with it. Meals are on time. The fire is lit. A holiday somewhere noisy, once in a while, would remind you what you are being quiet for.

=fortune opposition vesta
One partner tends the flame while the other enjoys the warmth.
Vesta is at one end of this, keeping something going by steady, unglamorous attention. The Part of Fortune at the other end receives the benefit. Resentment is the obvious risk: I do the work and you have the nice life. It is seldom that simple. Still, the one in the deckchair should take a turn at the hearth without being asked.
