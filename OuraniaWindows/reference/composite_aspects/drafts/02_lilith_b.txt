=lilith conjunction mc
This relationship is publicly known for what it refuses to go along with.
The Midheaven is reputation and shared direction in the world, and Lilith on it puts the pair's defiance in full view. The two of you may be seen as provocative, uncompromising, or simply difficult, and some of your standing rests on exactly that. It suits work that challenges convention. It sits badly with any setting that rewards compliance. Sooner or later the partnership has to decide what it is willing to be known for, and accept the cost.

=lilith sextile mc
The pair's unconventional streak can be put to work in its public life.
Lilith is the part of the relationship that does not conform, and the Midheaven is its standing and vocation. With a sextile the first can serve the second on request. A blunt opinion, a refusal to flatter, or a willingness to take up an unpopular subject can advance the two of you when chosen deliberately. Nothing obliges you to use it. Kept back, it leaves the public face of the partnership more ordinary than its private one.

=lilith square mc
What this relationship is in private keeps undercutting where it wants to get to.
The Midheaven holds ambition and reputation. Lilith, at odds with it, will not dress for the occasion. The pair's rawness can break out at the moment of advancement, or the effort to look respectable can leave both of you feeling false. Authority figures are a recurring sore point. The way through is seldom to tidy the instinct away. It is to look for a public role wide enough to hold it.

=lilith trine mc
Being itself in public costs this relationship very little.
Lilith's independence and the Midheaven's worldly standing sit comfortably together. The two of you can hold a visible position, or pursue a shared ambition, without playing a part to do it, and people tend to respect the lack of pretence. Reputation follows from character here. The danger is assuming that will always be so. A setting that does punish frankness may come as a shock to a pair used to being rewarded for it.

=lilith opposition mc
The private depths of this relationship pull against its public ambitions.
Opposite the Midheaven, Lilith sits at the root of the chart, the place of home, family and what is kept behind the door. Something raw lives in the domestic foundation: an old family taboo, a fierce need for privacy, a household that will not be supervised. It tugs at career and reputation, and each of you may side with one end. Neither end wins for long. The outer life stands better once the hidden one has been faced.

=lilith conjunction moon
Feeling in this relationship is fierce, private and not open to negotiation.
The Moon is the emotional home the two of you make. Lilith joined to it brings the wild thing indoors. Needs are intense, moods run deep, and there is a hunger for closeness mixed with a dread of being swallowed or cast out. The bond can be unusually strong for it. Possessiveness, sudden withdrawal and old fears of rejection come with the same contact, so safety here has to be built on plain words about what each of you needs.

=lilith sextile moon
This relationship can make room at home for feelings that are not pretty.
The Moon is the pair's comfort and care, and Lilith holds the emotions usually kept out of sight: rage, envy, hunger. The sextile offers a way to bring those in without wrecking the peace. When one of you admits to something ugly, the other can usually take it. It requires the admission. Unspoken, the same feelings wait outside, and the home stays pleasant and a little shallow.

=lilith square moon
The need for safety and the need to be untamed fight each other here.
The Moon wants a settled nest. Lilith will not be domesticated. Squared, comfort can feel like a cage to one of you while independence feels like abandonment to the other, and the positions change sides. Emotional scenes blow up around home, food, family and who looks after whom. Underneath lies a fear that needing someone means losing oneself. The tension eases when each of you can ask for closeness and for space without apology.

=lilith trine moon
Deep and difficult feelings are simply part of home in this relationship.
Lilith's intensity and the Moon's care run together. The two of you can be needy, angry, sexual or sad in front of each other without it shaking the foundation, and that gives the bond an unusual emotional honesty. Instinct guides the household more than convention does. A comfortable acceptance of strong feeling can turn into indulging it, though. Moods that are always welcome are not always examined, and sometimes they should be.

=lilith opposition moon
One of you holds the home and the other holds what will not be housed.
With the Moon opposite Lilith, care and wildness stand at two ends. One partner tends to become the provider of comfort, the other the one who bolts, rages or refuses to be looked after. Each resents the role and depends on the other playing theirs. The split is the difficulty, more than either person. It mends when the nurturer admits to a wild streak and the wild one admits to wanting a home.

=lilith conjunction neptune
In this relationship the forbidden and the ideal blur into one.
Neptune dissolves edges and longs for something beyond the ordinary. Lilith is raw instinct and the thing nobody admits to. Together they give the partnership a haunting, hard-to-name quality: strong fantasy, strong attraction to what is hidden, and a gift for art or for the uncanny. It is also easy to be deceived here, by each other or by yourselves. Longing can dress up a compulsion as a calling, so a few plain facts need keeping in view.

=lilith sextile neptune
Imagination gives this relationship a way to handle what it cannot say outright.
Lilith holds the pair's raw and unacceptable material, and Neptune offers image, music, ritual and sympathy. Linked by a sextile, the difficult thing can be approached sideways, through something made or shared, and compassion can soften what would otherwise be harsh. It is an opportunity, and it asks to be taken up. A creative or contemplative practice done together is the natural place for it.

=lilith square neptune
Raw instinct and wishful thinking confuse each other in this relationship.
Neptune would rather not look, and Lilith will not be ignored. In a square the two of you may idealise the bond while something ungoverned works underneath, or act on an urge and then tell yourselves a kinder story about it. Secrets, evasions and a sense of being misled are the usual signs. Guilt and blame drift without settling anywhere. Clear, unglamorous honesty is the remedy, taken in small regular doses.

=lilith trine neptune
This relationship has an easy, almost mystical acceptance of its own depths.
Lilith's instinct and Neptune's imagination move together. The two of you may share vivid dreams, a strong erotic or artistic current, and a sense that the bond reaches somewhere words do not. Shame has little hold. That is a gift, and it can make reality look optional. Boundaries blur pleasantly, practical matters slide, and what is called surrender may sometimes be drift.

=lilith opposition neptune
One of you speaks for the dream and the other for the thing the dream leaves out.
Neptune's ideal faces Lilith's raw truth. In this relationship one partner tends to carry the faith, the forgiveness and the romance, while the other carries the anger, the appetite and the refusal to pretend. The idealist feels dragged down and the realist feels lied to. Each is holding half of something whole. Love that has made room for the unlovely part is a good deal sturdier than the dream alone.

=lilith conjunction north_node
This relationship grows by becoming less tame.
The North Node shows the direction a partnership is meant to develop in, and Lilith on it makes that direction an uncomfortable one. The two of you are asked to stop accommodating, to say what you actually want, and to risk disapproval. It rarely feels safe. Each step toward it tends to be right all the same, and the periods when the pair is most itself are the ones that move it forward. Old habits of pleasing others are what get left behind.

=lilith sextile north_node
Being more honest and less compliant will help this relationship get where it is going.
The North Node marks the pair's line of growth and Lilith its unvarnished instinct. A sextile makes the instinct a useful ally when it is consulted. In moments of decision, the choice that feels a little too bold is often the one that serves the future. This is help on offer and nothing more forceful than that. The two of you can equally well ignore it and take the cautious route.

=lilith square north_node
The pull toward growth and the pull toward doing as it pleases cross each other here.
Lilith squares both ends of the nodal axis, so she fits neither the familiar past nor the intended future. The two of you may feel that your rawest instincts are a detour: too wild for the comfortable pattern, and no help with the new one. Outbursts tend to come at turning points. It is worth asking, each time, what the instinct is protecting. Often it is something the growth plan forgot to include.

=lilith trine north_node
What is most instinctive in this relationship points the same way as its growth.
Lilith's independence supports the North Node's direction without strain. When the two of you trust your gut and decline to be managed, things tend to move forward, and the future feels like an extension of who you already are. That is fortunate. It can also make development seem effortless when part of it does need work, especially the part that asks you to leave old comforts at the other end of the axis.

=lilith opposition north_node
The untamed side of this relationship belongs to where it has been.
Opposite the North Node, Lilith sits with the South Node, the place of habit and old ground. Defiance, fierce self-protection and a readiness to walk out are well practised here, perhaps from each of your histories. They come easily and they hold the pair back. The direction of growth lies in the opposite qualities, whatever the chart shows them to be. The old strength need not be thrown away, only relied on less.

=lilith sextile pluto

It is worth knowing it is there, since many couples have to face upheaval without it.

Jealousy and suspicion flare, and each may test the other's limits to see what holds. It is exhausting, and it reveals a great deal. =lilith trine pluto

The ease carries a risk of its own: a taste for extremity can become a habit, and ordinary contentment may be undervalued because it lacks an edge.

=lilith opposition pluto
One of you holds the power and the other refuses it.
Pluto's control and Lilith's defiance sit at opposite ends. Between the partners this easily becomes a ruler and a rebel, or a keeper of secrets and a breaker of them. Each provokes the other into the role. The struggle can run for years, changing subject while keeping its shape. It loosens when the one with the upper hand admits to fear, and the one resisting admits to wanting some of that power too.

=lilith trine saturn

=lilith opposition saturn

=lilith trine uranus

=lilith opposition uranus

=lilith trine venus

=lilith opposition venus

Venus and Lilith stand opposite. One partner tends to carry sweetness, tact and the wish to please, while the other carries appetite, bluntness and suspicion of anything too smooth. Each finds the other faintly dishonest or faintly cruel. They are two halves of one way of loving. It rights itself when the agreeable one risks a demand and the demanding one risks a kindness.
