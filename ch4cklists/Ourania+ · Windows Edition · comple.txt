Ourania+ · Windows Edition · completion audit

What Ourania still needs
An itemized survey of the gap between the engine and the application, measured against the source tree rather than estimated — and against what comparable desktop astrology software ships as a matter of course.

Audited 2 Sep 2026
Engine 52 astro classes
Suites 24 · 725,224 checks
Repo Tohickon/Ourania
The engine is years ahead of the application
This is not a half-built calculator. Zodiacal releasing to four levels, secondary progressions, solar arc, returns, profections, midpoints, dignities, sect, decans, lunar mansions, transfer of light, harmonic charts — all computed, all covered by check suites, much of it unreachable from the interface.

Counting references from the gui package to each engine class: five capabilities have zero. Midpoints, Rulership, DecanSystem, HarmonicResonance and Calibration are computed, correct, checked, and have no door. Several more have exactly one reference — reachable only as a side effect of something else.

That is this project's signature defect and it has now been logged four times: the browsable index whose only two entry points were both inside a panel that had to be open first; SavedCharts, a working profile store the "Name List" screen never once read; the harmonic dial the work plan still lists as unimplemented; and these five. The single highest-value work in this list is not new computation. It is doors.

A
Astronomy & correctness
14 items
The foundation everything else inherits. An error here is invisible and contaminates every reading downstream.

A1
Swiss Ephemeris integration
Built
Bundled, path-resolved through Ephemeris.PATH with property and environment overrides.
A2
Moshier fallback disclosure
Partial
Ephemeris.present() exists but nothing calls it to decide anything. If the ephemeris directory is wrong the app silently falls back to lower precision. The user is never told.
A3
House systems
Partial
Six offered (Placidus, Koch, Equal, Whole Sign, Campanus, Regiomontanus). Comparable apps ship 12–20: Porphyry, Alcabitius, Morinus, Topocentric, Meridian, Krusinski.
A4
Extreme-latitude house failure
Missing
Placidus and Koch are undefined above roughly 66°. No detection, no fallback, no warning — the chart will simply be wrong for a polar birth.
A5
Sidereal zodiac & ayanamsa
Missing
Tropical only. Sidereal with a selectable ayanamsa (Lahiri, Fagan-Bradley, Krishnamurti, Raman) is standard, and mandatory for any Vedic use.
A6
Heliocentric charts
Missing
Geocentric only.
A7
Topocentric positions
Partial
ChartFrame.compute takes a topocentric flag and an altitude; nothing in the UI sets either.
A8
True vs mean node, true vs mean Lilith
Built
Settings-backed variant toggles.
A9
Timezone & DST from history
Partial
Zone resolved from the geocoded location. Historic DST rules, war time, and pre-1970 local mean time are where birth charts silently go wrong by an hour; no evidence of a historic-rules test corpus.
A10
Atlas / geocoding
Partial
Geocoder exists and resolves names online. No offline atlas, so the app cannot cast a chart without a network — every comparable product ships one.
A11
Declination & out-of-bounds
Partial
ChartFrame.Body carries dec and outOfBounds. No declination chart, no parallel/contraparallel aspects, no out-of-bounds report.
A12
Antiscia & contra-antiscia
Missing
Standard in traditional practice, which this app otherwise serves well.
A13
Fixed stars
Missing
No star catalogue, no conjunctions to stars, no parans. Swiss Ephemeris supplies the data already.
A14
Asteroid breadth
Partial
29 registry points including Eris, Eros, Hygiea, Nessus, Pholus. Psyche is documented as blocked on an unsourceable ephemeris file — a real, recorded limit rather than an oversight.

If only five things get done
Push to GitHub (K3). Five commits and an irreplaceable corpus exist on one machine with no off-site copy. A vault file was destroyed today with no recovery path; the same exposure applies to everything else.
Settle the Swiss Ephemeris and corpus licensing (K5, K6). Every other decision about distribution depends on the answer, and the cost of finding out late is total.
Build the doors (D5, D6, D7, B8, C3–C7). Midpoints, rulership, decans, harmonic resonance, progressions, solar arc, returns and profections are computed and checked. This is the highest ratio of value to effort in the whole list.
Ship output (H1, H2, H3). No image, no print, no PDF. Nothing leaves the app, which caps its usefulness regardless of how good the engine is.
Transit search across a date range (C2). The one feature a practitioner opens comparable software to use, and the engine already computes everything it needs.
